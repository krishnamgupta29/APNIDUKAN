# 🛍️ ApniDukan Shahjahanpur

## 📖 Overview

ApniDukan Shahjahanpur is a modern local e-commerce platform designed to provide customers with a seamless online shopping experience. The platform enables users to discover products, place orders, track deliveries, submit feedback, and shop conveniently through both a website and mobile application.

Built with a focus on accessibility, convenience, and local commerce, ApniDukan aims to simplify online shopping for customers while creating a digital marketplace experience tailored for Shahjahanpur.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Many customers in smaller cities face challenges such as:

- Limited access to diverse products
- Lack of dedicated local online shopping platforms
- Delayed deliveries from large marketplaces
- Difficulty finding unique and trending products
- Limited digital presence of local businesses

ApniDukan addresses these challenges by offering a dedicated local e-commerce ecosystem.

---

# 💡 Solution

ApniDukan provides:

- Easy online shopping
- Local product discovery
- Fast order processing
- Delivery within 24 hours (where applicable)
- Mobile and web accessibility
- Customer feedback system
- Order tracking functionality
- Admin-controlled inventory and order management
